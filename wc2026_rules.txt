        2026 FIFA WORLD CUP — TOURNAMENT RULES & FORMAT
============================================================================
Source: FIFA Official Rules + Project Design Documents
Compiled: 2026-06-07
============================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
-----------
- Host countries: United States, Canada, Mexico (tri-host)
- Total teams: 48 (expanded from 32)
- Total matches: 104
- Tournament dates: June 11 – July 19, 2026

============================================================================

2. GROUP STAGE FORMAT
---------------------
- Number of groups: 12 (Groups A through L)
- Teams per group: 4
- Total group stage teams: 48
- Matches per group: 6 (each team plays 3 matches — round-robin)
- Total group stage matches: 72

Group Composition (from project data):
  Group A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, L
  Each group contains 4 teams seeded by FIFA ranking.

============================================================================

3. GROUP STAGE ADVANCEMENT RULES
---------------------------------
From each group of 4 teams:

  ADVANCE DIRECTLY:
  - 1st place (12 group winners)    → Round of 32
  - 2nd place (12 runners-up)       → Round of 32

  BEST THIRD-PLACE ADVANCEMENT:
  - 8 best 3rd-place teams (out of 12) → Round of 32

  TOTAL ADVANCING: 12 + 12 + 8 = 32 teams

  ELIMINATED:
  - 4th place from all groups (12 teams)
  - 4 worst 3rd-place teams (4 teams)
  - Total eliminated in group stage: 16 teams

Best 3rd-Place Selection Criteria:
  The 12 third-place teams are ranked by:
    1. Points (most)
    2. Goal difference (best)
    3. Goals scored (most)
    4. Fair play points (if still tied)
    5. Drawing of lots (last resort)
  Top 8 of the 12 qualify for R32.

============================================================================

4. GROUP STAGE TIEBREAKERS
---------------------------
Teams in the same group are ranked by (in order):

  1. Points (3 for win, 1 for draw, 0 for loss)
  2. Overall goal difference
  3. Overall goals scored (total goals for)
  4. Head-to-head points (among tied teams only)
  5. Head-to-head goal difference (among tied teams only)
  6. Head-to-head goals scored (among tied teams only)
  7. Fair play points:
       - Yellow card: -1 point
       - Indirect red (2 yellows): -3 points
       - Direct red: -4 points
       - Yellow + direct red: -5 points
  8. Drawing of lots by FIFA Organising Committee

Note: The project's simplified simulation uses criteria 1-3 only
(Points → GD → GF). Full tiebreakers 4-8 are implemented in the
official FIFA regulations.

============================================================================

5. KNOCKOUT STAGE STRUCTURE
----------------------------
Total knockout matches: 32

  Stage          Matches    Teams Remaining
  ─────────────  ───────    ───────────────
  Round of 32    16         32 → 16
  Round of 16     8         16 → 8
  Quarter-finals  4          8 → 4
  Semi-finals     2          4 → 2
  Third-place     1          2 (losers of SF)
  Final           1          2 → Champion

  Total knockout: 16 + 8 + 4 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 32 matches
  Total tournament: 72 (group) + 32 (knockout) = 104 matches

============================================================================

6. KNOCKOUT BRACKET — R32 PAIRING RULES
-----------------------------------------
The 32 qualifiers are paired as follows:

  STRUCTURE:
  The 12 groups are organized into 6 pairs:
    Pair 1: Groups A & B
    Pair 2: Groups C & D
    Pair 3: Groups E & F
    Pair 4: Groups G & H
    Pair 5: Groups I & J
    Pair 6: Groups K & L

  CROSS-PAIRING (within each group pair):
  For each pair of groups (e.g., A & B):
    - Winner of Group A vs Runner-up of Group B
    - Winner of Group B vs Runner-up of Group A
  This accounts for 12 R32 matches (6 pairs × 2 matches each).

  THIRD-PLACE ASSIGNMENT:
  The 8 best third-place teams are assigned to face:
    - Remaining group winners who don't already have a runner-up opponent
    - Some runners-up who don't already have a winner opponent
  The exact assignment depends on WHICH 8 third-place teams qualify
  (FIFA publishes a pre-defined assignment table based on the
  combination of groups from which 3rd-place teams qualify).

  BRACKET PROPAGATION:
  R32 winners advance to R16 → R16 winners to QF → QF to SF → SF to Final
  Adjacent R32 matches feed into the same R16 match:
    - R32 Match 1 winner vs R32 Match 2 winner = R16 Match 1
    - R32 Match 3 winner vs R32 Match 4 winner = R16 Match 2
    ... and so on.

============================================================================

7. KNOCKOUT MATCH RULES
------------------------
All knockout matches must produce a winner:

  90 Minutes:
  - Standard 90 minutes + stoppage time
  - If a winner is determined → match ends

  Extra Time (if drawn after 90 min):
  - Two periods of 15 minutes each (30 minutes total)
  - Golden goal rule is NOT used (full 30 minutes played)
  - If winner determined in extra time → match ends

  Penalty Shootout (if still drawn after extra time):
  - Best of 5 penalties each
  - If still tied after 5 → sudden death
  - Coin toss decides which team takes first penalty

============================================================================

8. SPECIAL RULES & NOTES
-------------------------
  a) HOST NATIONS:
     - USA, Canada, Mexico all automatically qualify
     - USA is the primary host (hosts majority of matches)
     - Mexico hosts opening match at Estadio Azteca
     - Final at MetLife Stadium, New Jersey

  b) VENUE DISTRIBUTION:
     - 16 host cities across 3 countries
     - USA: 11 cities
     - Canada: 2 cities (Toronto, Vancouver)
     - Mexico: 3 cities (Mexico City, Guadalajara, Monterrey)

  c) SQUAD SIZE:
     - 26 players per squad (expanded from traditional 23)
     - Up to 26 players can be named for each match
     - 5 substitutions allowed per match (from a bench of up to 15)

  d) MATCH SCHEDULING:
     - Group stage: 3 matchdays per group
     - All groups play simultaneously on final matchday (MD3)
     - No team plays twice within 3 days

  e) VAR (Video Assistant Referee):
     - Used in all 104 matches
     - Semi-automated offside technology (SAOT) deployed

  f) COOLING BREAKS:
     - Mandatory cooling breaks in matches with high heat/humidity
     - Two breaks per half (each 3 minutes) if temperature > 32°C
